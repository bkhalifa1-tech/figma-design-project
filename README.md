## 📄 Description

Janini is a pregnancy companion application designed to support and guide women throughout their pregnancy journey in a simple, safe, and user-friendly way.

The app allows pregnant women to track their pregnancy week by week, providing clear and simplified information about fetal development along with general, non-medical advice to ensure comfort and awareness.

One of the key features of the application is enabling the partner (husband) to participate in the journey through a shared account, where he can view progress updates and receive suggestions on how to support his partner.

The application also includes daily and weekly reminders for medical checkups and healthy habits, as well as features to track mood and daily activities.

Janini does not replace professional medical consultation, but acts as an educational and organizational companion during pregnancy.
